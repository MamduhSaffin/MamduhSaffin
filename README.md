## What I'm building

### Digital products & education

- **[TEMAN Haramain](https://teman.tgpu.my)** — A multilingual Hajj and Umrah companion focused on practical pilgrim assistance, location sharing, and senior-friendly safety features. [Public repository](https://github.com/MamduhSaffin/TEMAN-Haramain-by-TGPU)
- **[TGPU Iqra](https://iqra.tgpu.my)** — A mobile-first Arabic literacy and Iqra learning app. Foundational lessons and learning tools are under active beta development.
- **[SATU](https://satu.tgpu.my)** — An offline-first productivity tool for Malaysian micro-sellers, covering everyday records, calculations, invoices, and receipts. [Public repository](https://github.com/MamduhSaffin/SATU)
- **[Khalifah Kecil](https://khalifah.tgpu.my)** — A Malay-language children's learning experience supporting curiosity and real-world exploration. [Public repository](https://github.com/MamduhSaffin/khalifah-kecil)
- **[TGPU Learning Hub](https://learning.tgpu.my)** and **[TGPU Academy](https://academy.tgpu.my)** — Separate initiatives for open learning resources and structured educational programmes.

### Business & market-entry technology

- **[TGPU GCC Connect](https://connect.tgpu.my)** — A managed business-connection and execution-coordination platform for Malaysia–Saudi Arabia and wider GCC opportunities. Source code is private.
- **[TGPU Gulf Advisory](https://tgpugulf.com)** — Market-entry advisory, business development, and commercial-partner coordination between Malaysia and Saudi Arabia/GCC markets.
- **[GCC Market Entry](https://gccmarketentry.me)** — A multilingual educational resource for exploring routes to market, readiness, and regulatory considerations. [Public repository](https://github.com/MamduhSaffin/sell-to-the-middle-east)
